# Where the art comes from

Two sources, two licences, and the terms of both are met here rather than
assumed. A fan project has no excuse for treating somebody else's work the way
it hopes nobody treats its own.

## Pokémon sprites — PMD Sprite Collab

Every creature sprite in this game (`client/public/atlas/*.png`) comes from
**PMD Sprite Collab**, the community project that draws and maintains Pokémon
Mystery Dungeon style sprites.

- Source: <https://sprites.pmdcollab.org/>
- Repository: <https://github.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab>
- Licence: **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0**
  (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/>)

The licence asks for two things and both apply:

**Attribution.** Credited in the game itself — on the menu and in the field
guide — not only in this file. A credit nobody sees is not a credit.

**Non-commercial.** Clash of Pokémon is free. There are no advertisements, no
purchases, no subscriptions, no donations and nothing is sold. If that ever
changes, this art cannot come with it.

**Changes made.** The sprites are used as drawn; what changed is packaging.
Individual frames were repacked into texture atlases and their animation data
converted to the format Phaser reads. No sprite was redrawn, recoloured or
edited.

The sprites are the work of many individual artists, credited per-Pokémon in
the upstream repository. The full list lives there and is authoritative:
<https://sprites.pmdcollab.org/>

## Towers — Foozle

`foozle-spire-tower-01.png`, Spire Tower Pack 1, commissioned from Baldur and
distributed by Foozle. Released **CC0**, which asks for nothing. See
`foozle-LICENSE.txt`. Credited anyway, because taking free work silently is
still taking it silently.
